Our Mission

Bridging the Gap Between Farm and Market

Farmers often face limited bargaining power and dependence on intermediaries when bringing their produce to market. Buyers, meanwhile, may have limited direct access to farmers and transparent crop pricing.

CropSync creates a direct digital connection between both sides — giving farmers greater control over how they sell and giving buyers a transparent way to discover and request produce.

The Challenge

Why Farmers Struggle Today

Key market obstacles that hinder direct agricultural trade and fair pricing.

Middlemen Dependency

Farmers may depend on multiple intermediaries to reach buyers, reducing their share of the final value.

Limited Price Transparency

Farmers may not have clear visibility into reference prices when negotiating crop prices.

Weak Direct Market Access

Small farmers can find it difficult to reach consumers and bulk buyers directly.

Limited Buyer Visibility

Buyers may struggle to discover available produce directly from farmers in nearby locations.

Pricing Protection

Why MSP Matters

CropSync displays Government Minimum Support Price (MSP) reference values alongside applicable crops to provide greater pricing awareness and help farmers make more informed listing decisions.

* Government MSP serves as a reference benchmark to enhance price transparency. CropSync does not mandate or set government prices.
