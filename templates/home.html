Our Mission

Bridging the Gap Between Farm and Market

Farmers often face limited bargaining power and dependence on intermediaries when bringing their produce to market. Buyers, meanwhile, may have limited direct access to farmers and transparent crop pricing.

CropSync creates a direct digital connection between both sides — backed by transparent MSP reference pricing, 1-click Tamil language translation for farmers, and an integrated logistics tracking pipeline that manages pickup, door-to-door transit, and verified payment settlement.

The Challenge

Why Farmers Struggle Today

Key market obstacles that hinder direct agricultural trade and fair pricing.

Middlemen Dependency

Farmers may depend on multiple intermediaries to reach buyers, reducing their share of the final value.

Limited Price Transparency

Farmers may not have clear visibility into reference prices when negotiating crop prices.

Weak Direct Market Access

Small farmers can find it difficult to reach consumers and bulk buyers directly.

Limited Buyer Visibility

Buyers may struggle to discover available produce directly from farmers in nearby locations.

Platform Features

Everything Needed for Direct Trade

Designed for simplicity, transparency, and rural accessibility.

Farmer Dashboard & தமிழ் Support

Manage listings and orders easily. Includes a 1-click English/Tamil toggle (English / தமிழ்) with dual-language crop titles like Rice (அரிசி) for effortless local trading.

Buyer Marketplace

Search and filter fresh produce by crop and location, compare listing prices against official MSP reference benchmarks, and place orders with direct address transfer.

MSP-Aware Pricing

Government Minimum Support Price reference benchmarks are prominently displayed alongside listings to ensure fair compensation and eliminate underpricing.

Integrated Logistics & Tracking

Dedicated logistics handles pickup, door-to-door transit, and live 6-stage order tracking (Pickup Scheduled → Picked Up → In Transit → Out for Delivery → Delivered → Settled).

Real-Time Email Updates

Automated order and logistics tracking emails keep farmers and buyers instantly informed at every stage from order acceptance to final payout.

Secured Payment Settlement

Payment is collected upon delivery and safely settled directly to the farmer, completing the trade cycle with full integrity and confirmation.

Pricing Protection

Why MSP Matters

CropSync displays Government Minimum Support Price (MSP) reference values alongside applicable crops to provide greater pricing awareness and help farmers make more informed listing decisions.
